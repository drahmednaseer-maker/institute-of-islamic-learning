Common questions before booking

How soon can we start?
Most students take their first class within 24 hours of booking. If you need a very specific slot it can take a day or two longer to match the right tutor.
Can I request a female teacher?
Yes — simply note it in the form and we will match you with a female tutor.
Do you teach adults who are complete beginners?
Every week. A large share of our adult students begin at the Qaida, and our tutors are used to teaching without any assumed background.
Which timezones do you cover?
All of them. Our tutors work across shifts covering North America, Europe, the Gulf, South Asia and Australia.
