✦ Fee questions

The details, up front

Is there a registration fee?
No. There is no admission fee, no material charge and no platform fee — the monthly tuition is the only cost.
Do you offer a sibling discount?
Yes — 10% off every student's fee when two or more from the same family enrol. It's applied automatically to your invoice.
Can we pause during holidays?
Absolutely. Tell us the dates and we'll pause your schedule and your billing, then hold your slot for your return.
What if I want to stop?
Message us and billing stops at the end of the classes already taught. There is no contract and no notice period.
Are the trial classes really free?
Yes — three full one-to-one classes with no card details taken and no obligation to continue.
