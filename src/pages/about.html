About the Institute

We exist for one purpose: to put a qualified, patient Quran teacher within reach of every Muslim family, wherever they live.

✦ Our story

Founded by teachers, not by marketers

The Institute began in 2016 with three teachers and a handful of students in the UK and the USA who could not find a local madrasah that suited their schedule. Word travelled between families, and it kept travelling.

Today more than 12,000 students have studied with us across 45 countries — but the model has not changed. Every lesson is still one-to-one. Every teacher is still auditioned by another teacher. And every student still starts with a free assessment and an honest plan, even when that plan says the road will be long.

✦ What we stand by

Four promises we don't break

Authenticity first

We teach Hafs 'an 'Asim as it was transmitted, from teachers with verified chains — no shortcuts and no invented methods.

Patience over pace

A student who is rushed learns to fear the Mushaf. We move at the speed the student can actually hold.

Honest reporting

If progress stalls we say so, and we change the plan or the teacher. Parents get the truth, not reassurance.

Safety for children

Every tutor is background-checked, classes may be recorded on request, and parents are welcome in any lesson.
