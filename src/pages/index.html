✦ Who we are

A patient teacher, a clear plan, and the Book of Allah

The Institute of Islamic Learning was founded on a simple belief: every Muslim deserves a teacher who listens carefully, corrects gently, and never rushes. Whether you are opening the Qaida for the first time or completing your Hifz, you learn one-to-one — never lost in a crowded class.

  • Structured curriculum — every student follows a written plan with clear milestones, from letters to fluent recitation.
  • Teachers who specialise — a Hifz specialist for memorisation, a Tajweed specialist for pronunciation, a kids' specialist for young learners.
  • Parents stay informed — join the class, read the monthly report, and message the tutor whenever you need.

✦ Questions, answered

Before you book

Is the trial really free?
Yes — three complete one-to-one classes with a qualified tutor, at no cost. We don't ask for card details, and there's nothing to cancel if you decide not to continue.
Can we request a female teacher?
Of course. Tell us your preference when you book and we'll match you with a female tutor. You can change tutor at any point, free of charge.
What ages do you teach?
From four-year-olds starting the Qaida through to adults beginning from zero. Our children's tutors are specifically trained for short attention spans and playful, patient teaching.
What if we miss a class?
Message your coordinator on WhatsApp and we'll reschedule. You are only ever billed for classes that actually take place.
How do we pay, and can we stop?
Fees are billed monthly by bank transfer, PayPal, Wise or card. There is no contract and no notice period — pause or stop whenever you need to.
Do you teach in languages other than English?
Yes. Our tutors also teach in Urdu and Arabic, and we have teachers who work with French, German and Turkish speakers.

Your first three classes are on us

Tell us the student's age, level and the times that suit you. We'll confirm your tutor on WhatsApp, usually the same day.
